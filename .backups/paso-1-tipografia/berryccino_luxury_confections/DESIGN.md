## Brand & Style

The brand conveys romantic luxury, artisanal indulgence, and celebratory gifting. It caters to discerning gift-givers, event planners, and epicureans seeking statement centerpieces—chocolate-dipped strawberry towers, floral-fruit arrangements, and curated sweet boxes. The emotional response is sensory, lavish, warm, and sophisticated.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Editorial Luxury** with **Subtle Tactile Warmth**. It eschews stark, cold minimalism in favor of warm champagne surfaces, deep cocoa typography, rich strawberry blush tones, and polished warm gold accents. High-contrast typography pairs classical high-fashion serif elegance with crisp, contemporary legibility. Visual depth is rendered through luminous cream backdrops, soft atmospheric shadows, and deliberate whitespace framing rich culinary photography.

## Colors

The palette balances mouth-watering richness with haute-confectionery elegance:

- **Primary (`strawberry-rose` / #D84A6E):** The signature vibrant berry tone. Used selectively for primary call-to-actions, active state tabs, dynamic pricing emphasis, and key conversion paths.
- **Secondary (`chocolate-espresso` / #2B1E1A):** Rich, bitter-sweet dark cacao. Replaces pure black to deliver soft, high-contrast, editorial text hierarchy, navigation links, and structural borders.
- **Tertiary (`accent-gold` / #C5A059):** Polished vintage gold. Applied to luxury crests, specialty badges, star ratings, personalized gifting tags, and delicate hairline separators.
- **Neutral (`cream-champagne` / #FAF6F0 & `cream-surface` / #FDFBF7):** Silk-like parchment and ivory tones forming warm canvas foundations, elevating food photography without sterile glare.
- **Supporting Accents:** `strawberry-blush` (#F1B2BD) for soft interactive hover fills, seasonal chips, and pill containers; `chocolate-deep` (#1C1311) for footers and editorial dark moments.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes haute-couture editorial presence with razor-sharp e-commerce clarity:

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set in **Playfair Display**, lending literary romanticism, generous tall ascenders, and delicate serifs to product names, hero banners, and section divides.
- **Body, UI, & Numbers:** Rendered in **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering open counters, modern geometric balance, and immaculate legibility for prices, ingredient descriptions, and nutritional specifications.
- **Casing & Spacing:** Use `label-caps` (all-caps with wide tracking: 0.12em) for metadata labels, category tags, badge prefixes, and delivery statuses to evoke refined packaging typography.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a flexible 12-column grid system designed around breathing room and visual composure.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, centered container capped at `1360px`, with 3rem page margins. Product grids default to 3 or 4 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem (`20px`) gutters, 2rem page margins. Product grids reflow into 2 or 3 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem (`16px`) gutters, 1.25rem page margins. Product grids collapse into a crisp 2-column or 1-column featured card presentation with horizontal carousel options for collections.
- **Rhythm:** Generous vertical sections separated by `space-2xl` (`64px`) to create salon-like cadence, avoiding dense warehouse-style catalog clusters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, low-opacity chocolate-tinted ambient shadows and subtle tonal layering rather than harsh gray drop-shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Surface color `cream-champagne` (#FAF6F0) or `cream-surface` (#FDFBF7).
- **Level 1 (Cards, Product Tiles):** Backed by `#FDFBF7`, framed with a delicate hairline border (`rgba(43, 30, 26, 0.06)`), and lifted with an ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(43, 30, 26, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Floating Quick-View, Dropdowns):** Smooth transition to `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(43, 30, 26, 0.10)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Header, Flyout Drawer, Modal Cart):** Frosted glass surface using backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), a 90% tinted `cream-surface` background, and depth shadow `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(43, 30, 26, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced, refined contour language (Roundedness 2):

- **Interactive Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Cards):** Base corner radius of `0.5rem` (`8px`) to maintain tailored luxury without feeling overly industrial or sharp.
- **Containers & Product Cards:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) for primary product tiles and media blocks, mimicking fine patisserie gift boxes.
- **Badges, Filter Chips & Floating Pill Buttons:** Full pill radii (`9999px`) to create soft, inviting touchpoints for curated tags, occasion filters, and floating cart indicators.

## Components

### Header & Navigation
- **Architecture:** Minimalist single-row navigation with centered logo or balanced split layout. Navigation links limited to: *Catálogo / Tienda*, *Ocasiones*, *Postres & Dulces*, and *Contacto*.
- **Utility Cluster:** Subdued search icon button, telephone ordering direct link (`+ Teléfono / Pedidos WhatsApp` encased in a subtle gold-bordered pill), and Cart trigger displaying item badge in `strawberry-rose`.
- **Visual Style:** Sticky translucent frosted bar with hairline chocolate bottom border (`rgba(43, 30, 26, 0.08)`).

### Product Cards (Boutique Presentation)
- **Imagery:** 4:5 vertical aspect ratio highlighting towering strawberry arrangements. Crisp zoom effect on hover (`scale(1.04)`, `400ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
- **Pill Category & Status Tags:** Placed top-left inside image (`Bestseller`, `Edición Limitada`, `Personalizable`) in soft cream or gold foil styles.
- **Card Body:** Centered or left-aligned with `headline-sm` product title in Playfair Display, subtle star rating row with gold stars, clear base price and customizable options indicator (`Desde $...`).
- **Actions:** Dual-action layout: Primary full-width `Añadir al carrito` button, or split button with secondary `Personalizar` pill opening topping/chocolate customization.

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary:** Filled `strawberry-rose` (#D84A6E) background, pure white text, font weight 600, `0.5rem` radius. Soft hover brightening to `#E86F88` with slight elevation lift.
- **Secondary / Luxury Action:** Deep `chocolate-espresso` (#2B1E1A) fill with white or gold typography for high-tier actions.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Hairline border (`1px solid rgba(43, 30, 26, 0.2)`), transparent background, chocolate text, tinting to `cream-subtle` on hover.

### Filter Tabs & Chips
- **Category Filter Rail:** Horizontally scrollable on mobile, flex-wrapped on desktop. Pill-shaped buttons with `0.5rem` vertical and `1.25rem` horizontal padding.
- **States:** Inactive chips styled with `cream-surface` background and light cocoa border; Active chips styled with solid `chocolate-espresso` or `strawberry-rose` background and crisp white text.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** Warm `cream-surface` background, 1px border (`#ECE8E8`), deep chocolate text, `0.5rem` rounded corners. Focus ring in 2px semi-transparent `strawberry-rose` (`rgba(216, 74, 110, 0.25)`).
- **Date/Time Delivery Picker:** Custom luxury calendar dropdown for scheduling occasion arrivals, styled with gold-highlighted delivery slots.

### Checkboxes & Radios (Add-on Customizer)
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular or soft-corner controls tinted in `strawberry-rose` upon selection, designed for chocolate dip variants (Dark, Milk, White, Ruby), edible gold leaf, and floral additions.
